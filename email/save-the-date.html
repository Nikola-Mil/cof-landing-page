Crna Gora se približava odlučujućem trenutku na svom evropskom putu.

Nobelovac, zajedno sa bivšim premijerima, ministrima, glavnim pregovaračima, visokim zvaničnicima i međunarodnim ekspertima iz devet država članica EU koji su svoje zemlje vodili kroz proces pristupanja Evropskoj uniji, okupiće se u Podgorici.

Montenegro in the EU

Lessons, Issues, and Policy Directions

A Nobel laureate and leaders of nine EU accession journeys come to Podgorica.

16 October 2026  |  Podgorica

 

Montenegro is approaching a defining moment on its European path.

A Nobel laureate, together with former prime ministers, ministers, chief negotiators, senior officials and international experts from nine EU Member States who led their countries through the EU accession process, will gather in Podgorica.

What does it take to successfully complete accession — and how to ensure continued prosperity after joining the EU?

Nine countries. First-hand experience. Lessons learned.

One conversation Montenegro needs now.
