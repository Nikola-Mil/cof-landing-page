SAVE THE DATE — MONTENEGRO IN THE EU
Lessons, Issues, and Policy Directions

A Nobel laureate and leaders of nine EU accession journeys come to Podgorica.

16 October 2026 | Podgorica

Montenegro is approaching a defining moment on its European path.

A Nobel laureate, together with former prime ministers, ministers, chief
negotiators, senior officials and international experts from nine EU Member States
who led their countries through the EU accession process, will gather in Podgorica.

What does it take to successfully complete accession — and how to ensure
continued prosperity after joining the EU?

Nine countries. First-hand experience. Lessons learned.
One conversation Montenegro needs now.
